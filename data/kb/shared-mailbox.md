---
title: Shared mailbox missing in Outlook
category: email
team: service_desk
---
Symptoms: shared mailbox not visible in Outlook, cannot send as the shared mailbox.

Steps:
1. Check Full Access and Send As permissions in the Exchange admin centre.
2. Automapping can take up to 60 minutes; restart Outlook afterwards.
3. If it still does not appear, add the mailbox manually under account settings.
