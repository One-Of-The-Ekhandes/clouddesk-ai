---
title: Service outage and major incidents
category: outage
team: incident_management
---
Symptoms: a production service is down or returning errors (for example HTTP 500 or 503), many users affected, customers affected.

Steps:
1. Confirm the impact: which service, how many users, since when.
2. Raise a P1 major incident and page the on-call engineer.
3. Post status updates every 30 minutes until resolved.
Outages affecting customers or many users are always P1 and always escalated.
