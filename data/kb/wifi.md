---
title: Office Wi-Fi not connecting
category: network
team: network_team
---
Symptoms: cannot join the corporate Wi-Fi, certificate warning, connected but no internet.

Steps:
1. Check whether other people on the same floor are affected.
2. Forget the network and reconnect; confirm the device certificate is valid.
3. A single user: service desk. A whole floor or building: escalate to the network team as a possible outage.
