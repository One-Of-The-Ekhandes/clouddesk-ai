---
title: MFA enrolment and lost authenticator device
category: account_access
team: service_desk
---
Symptoms: new phone, lost phone, MFA prompts not arriving, user never enrolled in MFA.

Steps:
1. Verify identity in person or by video call before resetting MFA methods.
2. Revoke the old authenticator method and active sessions.
3. Walk the user through registering the authenticator app again.
Accounts without MFA are a security risk: enrol them as a priority.
