---
title: VPN disconnects or will not connect
category: network
team: service_desk
---
Symptoms: VPN drops every few minutes, cannot connect from home, slow internal sites over VPN.

Steps:
1. Confirm the internet connection works without the VPN.
2. Update the VPN client to the current approved version.
3. Disable Wi-Fi adapter power saving and test on a wired connection.
4. If several users report the same problem at once, treat it as a possible outage and inform the network team.
